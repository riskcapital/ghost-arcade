# Ghost Arcade 2.1.0

## A welcome screen, a guided tour and ready-made shows

The first launch now opens on a welcome screen with four choices: take the tour, start from a template, open a project, or start blank. Nothing is forced: the tour and the templates are also in the shortcuts overlay (press ?) and the File menu.

- **Three tour paths.** VJ, projection mapping, or musician connecting a DAW. Each step opens the panel it talks about and points at the control it means.
- **Templates.** Band Stage, Club VJ, Psychedelic and Mapping Starter build a working show in one click: a full deck organised in style blocks, clips that react to audio, and Performer loaded with a full deck. **File → New from Template…** opens them at any time.
- Templates and the VJ tour open VJ mode live, so clips fire straight away.

## VJ decks of any size

- **Big decks stack properly.** A deck of about six rows or more showed only one row on the output instead of the whole stack. Every row now reaches the output with its transparency.
- **Sharper output.** Clips keep their full resolution, and the A/B crossfades and the VJ Mix render at the output resolution. Before, clips were shrunk to fit a 1024 pixel picture and the mix was stretched to the screen.
- **Up to 32 rows play at once,** the size of a full deck. Before, rows past the 16th played black.
- **Send the output to the projector from VJ mode.** A projector button sits left of REC. Click it to go fullscreen on the projector, click again to take it off, right-click to choose the display. Settings → Display opens with the same Output controls.
- **Composition effects work on the whole output.** Effects that read neighbouring pixels (ASCII, blur, kaleidoscope) now work in the Comp tab in every live VJ mode, not only on layers and clips.
- **Smoother A/B crossfades and faster faders.** Moving the crossfader no longer resends the whole deck, and a fader move redraws only the rows it touches.
- The deck toolbars and layer controls stay put while the grid scrolls, and the deck name sits beside LIVE in A/B mode.
- Several files dropped on a row fill that row, and reordering rows keeps every video on its row.

## Map Sim

- **Auto-map this model** builds a mapping for the demo model in one click, with every face that can be seen from the projector, and applies it to the editor.
- Mapping is more accurate on the museum facade and the fragment cube, surfaces default to dark grey, and the Map Sim runs at full frame rate.
- Grouping mapped surfaces to share one shader keeps every face covered.

## New looks

- **Interface languages:** Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese, in Settings → Appearance. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+E returns to English.
- **Stage light pack:** Beam Rig, Colour Wash, Light Curtain, Gobo Spots, Blinders, Liquid Sky, Followspot, Strobe and Mirror Ball, drawn on black so a projector across a band works as the lighting rig.
- **Psychedelic pack:** Oil Wheel, Acid Flow, Tie-Dye, Chrysanthemum, Form Constants and Oil Slick.
- **New GA2 shaders:** Circle Limit, Eclipse, Coloured Shadows, Lightbox, Phase Portrait, Soft Cells, Kakeya, Cap Set, Murmuration and Moving Sofa.
- **Defocus Bokeh** can keep a round area or a band sharp and soften the picture toward the edges, with Depth of Field, Soft Edges, Shallow Portrait and Focus Band presets.
- Layer shapes: the edge outline traces the same shape the mask draws, and star layers draw again.

## Performance

- The app no longer resends every layer to the renderer once a second while nothing changes.
- Layers that do not cover a pixel cost almost nothing, and a project can hold 1024 layers instead of 64.
- Grouped shader layers share one render, and the Stage Sim and Map Sim use the renderer's picture instead of drawing the whole project a second time.
- The phone link sends nothing when no phone is connected, and the phone app loads thumbnails for every shader.

## Linux

- The Output Preview and the Deck A and Deck B monitors show in VJ mode.
- When no GPU is drawing, Ghost Arcade checks the machine and says what is missing (usually the Vulkan driver), gives the command to install it, and offers Ghost Arcade 1.9, which runs without it.
