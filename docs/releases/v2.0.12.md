# Ghost Arcade 2.0.12

Masks on stage screens, Bezier warp, clip audio in your recordings, and
recordings that start when you press REC.

## Masks on stage screens

In Screens, select a screen and open Masks. Add a mask, click on the canvas to
place its points, then close it by clicking the first point, right-clicking or
pressing Enter. Each mask has Feather and Invert, and a screen can have up to 8.
Masks are cut after the screen's crop and warp, so they stay on the surface when
you re-pin it, and what the projector shows matches the preview.

Drag a point to move it, click a + on an edge to add one, and right-click a
point to remove it. Masks are saved with the project's screens.

## Bezier warp

Set a layer to Mesh warp and tick Bezier curves. Click a point to show its
handles, then drag a handle to bend the edge. Handles mirror each other,
Alt-drag unlinks them, and double-clicking a handle straightens it. The arrow
keys nudge the handle you clicked last.

A curved wall that needed a 12 × 12 mesh now maps with 4 × 4. The curves are
worked out on the GPU for every pixel, so they stay smooth at any resolution,
and they follow when you corner-pin the layer. Meshes without handles render
exactly as before.

## Clip audio in your recordings

VJ recordings now include the clip audio the audience hears: after clip and
layer volume, pan and mute, the A/B crossfader and the master volume, mixed
with any mic or system audio you record. It lines up with the picture to within
a frame. Recordings from 2.0.9 to 2.0.11 left it out.

## Audio follows clip transitions

When a layer transitions from one clip to the next, the audio now crossfades
over the same time at a constant loudness. If another trigger interrupts, it
carries on from where it is, with no jump. A 0 s transition is still a cut.

## Recordings

- **Recordings start when you press REC.** Short recordings used to lose up
  to 2 seconds at the start while the encoder got going. Frames are now kept
  from the moment you press REC.
- **Include Audio.** Settings > Recording > Include Audio turns sound off when
  you want a silent video file.

## Fixes

- **Clip audio no longer crackles.** Since 2.0.9, clip audio has had a faint
  crackle through the speakers, from a tiny jump in its playback position many
  times a second.

## Good to know

- Bezier warp works on layer meshes. Master Warp and screen meshes stay
  straight for now.
- Screen masks have straight edges and up to 32 points each, and undo doesn't
  cover them yet.
- Screens belong to the project, so save it to keep your masks.
- If the encoder is slow to start, the first second of a recording can show
  fewer distinct frames, but none of its length is lost.
- These features were tested on macOS. Windows builds use the same code but
  have not been run on a Windows machine yet.
