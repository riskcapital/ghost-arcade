# Ghost Arcade 2.0.13

The biggest mapping release yet. One-click beat-synced Looks, crisp vector
Edge Effects, live VJ playback on mapped surfaces, Art-Net and sACN in and out,
a show control suite with cues, timecode, a scheduler and projector power, and
3D projection mapping onto real objects. ISF shaders gain multiple passes and
live audio, and recordings can capture one layer or one screen, with
transparency. Plus per-screen warps with Bezier curves, curved screen masks,
painted masks, and undo for all of it.

## Edge Effects, crisp and complete

Edge Effects are now drawn straight onto the output after the warp, at output
resolution, so lines stay razor sharp under a corner pin or a mesh warp, at
1080p and 4K alike. Stroke widths are in output pixels by default, so a 4 px
line stays 4 px along a perspective edge, with Scale with surface as an option.
Dashes, trims and tracers are measured along the path, so they stay evenly
spaced on curves. Glows get a sharp core and a clean halo.

Every effect type now renders on the projector exactly as it looks in the
editor, and a layer can stack up to 16 of them, up from 4.

New looks:

- **Borders:** Blinking, Half, Quarter, and a travelling Line with Boomerang
  and Yoyo modes.
- **Fills:** angular and diamond gradients, Random colour, Inside, Outside,
  Corner, Swipe, Group swipe (one wipe across a whole group), Stairs, Hypnotic,
  Stripes, Double stripes, Mosaic, Halftone, Hatch, Grid, Pattern, Iris, Clock
  wipe and Scan line.
- **Structure:** Radial glow, Wireframe, Origami, Inner lines and Elastic, plus
  3D Flip X and Flip Y with perspective and a movable centre point.
- **Vector tools:** Trim path (draw on, draw off, boomerang), Offset outlines,
  Zig-zag, Wiggle, custom Dash patterns and Marching ants on the beat.
- **Accents:** Corner accents, Vertex dots with beat bursts, and a Comet.
- **Group chases:** any effect can run as a wave across a group of shapes, in
  layer order, left to right, or out from the centre.

Every numeric setting takes audio, LFO, beat and Auto modulation, keyframes and
macros.

**Presets:** save a layer's stack in Edge Effects > Presets, then apply it to
several selected layers at once, or add it to their stacks. Libraries import
and export between computers.

## Looks: a show in three clicks

- **Looks gallery.** Draw your shapes, click Looks, pick one. 22 beat-synced
  looks, including Neon Pulse, Kick Strobe, Comet Chase, Beat Step, Stripe
  Sweep, Halftone Breathe and Laser Grid, apply to every shape at once. They
  move on a tapped or typed tempo, so no microphone is needed. Also in VJ MAP
  mode.
- **Palettes.** Recolour a look with one of 8 palettes in one click. Applying
  or changing a look is one undo step, and picking another replaces it instead
  of stacking.
- **Colour chases.** Screen FX chases can drive colour as well as brightness,
  in two colours or a rainbow, and you can set the chase order by clicking
  screens in turn. Numbered badges show the order.
- **Looks in VJ MAP.** The Looks button in the VJ header dresses the map's
  surfaces live. The look stays on as you fire different presets, and Remove
  Look brings back each preset's own Edge Effects.

## Perform the map live

MAP mode in the VJ deck now plays the map like a VJ set.

- **One shared map.** Every mapping preset uses the same surfaces. Edit a warp
  once and every preset that uses that surface follows, even the one that's
  live. Detach a surface in the layer panel for a one-off warp in one preset.
- **Live sources on any surface.** Set a surface's Source to a VJ row, the deck
  mix or a VJ group, then fire clips on that row and they play on the surface
  beside your presets.
- **The whole launcher works in MAP.** Clip transitions, beat sync, autopilot,
  clip audio, deck B and the crossfader all work on MAP rows, and ordinary
  clips can be fired there too.
- Firing a preset no longer replaces the layers you're editing, and a preset's
  video keeps playing when a row switches between presets on the same surface.

Older projects are converted the first time they open, and nothing moves.

## Art-Net and sACN, out and in

**Pixel mapping.** Settings > Integrations > Pixel Mapping sends the
composition to LED fixtures over Art-Net or sACN (E1.31). Fixtures can be
strips, matrices (including zig-zag wiring) or custom points, up to 8192 pixels
each, in any common colour order including RGBW. Universes span automatically,
170 RGB or 128 RGBW pixels each, and a pixel never straddles two. Send by
Art-Net unicast or broadcast with optional ArtSync, or sACN multicast or
unicast, at up to 60 fps. Test buttons check the wiring, a readout flags
overlapping channels, and fixtures save with the project. Blackout, disabling a
fixture and quitting all send a clean black frame.

**LED FX on any fixture.** Art-Net and sACN fixtures can join LED groups and
run every LED FX pattern from the WLED panel, with blend modes, automation and
BPM.

**DMX input.** A lighting desk can now drive Ghost Arcade. Turn it on in
Settings > Integrations > DMX Input, pick a control, press Learn and move a
fader. Or press Cmd/Ctrl+M, click any control on screen and move a fader.
Faders can be 8-bit or 16-bit, buttons fire cleanly on a threshold, and HTP, LTP and sACN priority merging handle more than one desk.
Bindings save with the project; the input stays off until you turn it on on
each computer.

## Show control

For installs and shows that run without an operator.

- **Cue list.** Open Show, then Cues. Cues fire mapping presets, snapshots,
  clips, columns, blackout, macros, layer opacity, any control with a fade, the
  show timeline, or projector commands. Each cue waits for GO or follows on
  after a number of seconds or beats, then goes to the next cue, jumps, loops
  or ends. Markers on the timeline fire cues as it plays. GO, Back, Stop and
  Reset can be learned to MIDI and OSC.
- **Timecode.** Settings > Show Control > Timecode chases LTC from an audio
  input or MTC over MIDI, at 24, 25, 29.97 drop-frame or 30 fps, with an offset
  and freewheel. It can run the show timeline, fire cues at their timecode, or
  both.
- **Scheduler.** Weekly show times with date exceptions. At the start it can
  power the projectors on, open the outputs and fire a cue; at the end it can
  black out and power them off. A machine that wakes up mid-show starts
  playing.
- **Start at boot.** Launch at login, open a chosen project, open the outputs
  fullscreen and press GO, with no prompts in the way.
- **Projectors.** Settings > Show Control > Projectors controls PJLink
  projectors: power, shutter and status, from buttons, cues or the scheduler.
  Projector passwords are stored encrypted on the computer, never in the
  project file.

## 3D projection mapping

Map Sim now maps onto real objects, not only in preview.

- **Projector view output.** Set a Screen's Source to a Map Sim projector and
  it outputs exactly what that projector sees: your live show wrapped onto the
  3D model, with occlusion, crop and edge blend. "Content from" can lock the
  content to the model and throw it from any projector.
- **Point-matching calibration.** In Map Sim, choose Calibrate to real
  projector. Click a point on the model, drag the crosshair onto the same point
  on the real object, and repeat. From six points Ghost Arcade solves the
  projector's position, aim and lens and shows the error for each point in
  pixels. Each point is one undo step, and the calibration saves with the
  project.

## ISF shaders, complete

- **Multi-pass shaders render on the output.** Feedback trails, multi-stage
  blurs and effects that build up frame after frame now work, including
  half-size and high-precision buffers. Feedback starts clean whenever you
  reload the shader or change the output size.
- **Audio inputs react to your live audio.** ISF spectrum and waveform inputs
  now get real data, at the number of bands or samples each shader asks for.
  With no live audio they follow the overall bass, mid and high levels instead
  of freezing.
- Multi-pass and audio shaders also compile in the browser preview and
  thumbnails.

## Recording: one layer, one screen, with transparency

- **Pick what REC records.** The new arrow beside REC chooses the whole
  composition, one layer, one VJ layer or one Screen. A Screen recording
  captures exactly what that projector shows, after crop, warp, masks and edge
  blend, even when its window isn't open. Layers record on their own at full
  opacity.
- **Pro formats.** ProRes 422 HQ, ProRes 4444 with alpha, HAP and HAP Alpha,
  as MOV. H.264 MP4 stays the default. Layer and composition recordings in
  ProRes 4444 or HAP Alpha keep transparency, ready for compositing.
- Render to Video can write ProRes 422 HQ or HAP too, and clip audio goes into
  MOV recordings uncompressed.

## Warps and masks

- **Per-screen warps.** Each screen can be a Rectangle, Corner pin or Mesh
  again, so every projector in a multi-projector show gets its own keystone.
  Master Warp still shapes the whole output on top.
- **Bezier everywhere.** Screen meshes and Master Warp now take Bezier handles
  like layer meshes: tick Bezier curves, drag a point's handles, Alt-drag to
  unlink, double-click to straighten.
- **Curved screen masks.** Double-click a mask point to switch it between a
  corner and a curve, then drag its handles. The feather follows the curve.
- **Painted masks.** Every layer now has a Paint Mask: brush holes into it
  with Erase and bring them back with Restore, with brush size, softness and
  opacity. The painted hole stays on the same spot of the picture when you
  move, corner-pin or mesh-warp the layer, and edges stay smooth on a 4K
  projector. Each stroke is one undo step; Clear, Invert and Show/Hide are one
  click. X or Alt swaps Erase and Restore, [ and ] resize the brush.
- **Undo for screens and masks.** Cmd/Ctrl+Z now undoes screen crops, mask
  edits and Master Warp changes, in order with your other edits. Blackout and
  test patterns are never undone.

## Fixes

- **Clicking outside the crash-recovery box no longer deletes your unsaved
  project.** It used to count as Discard; now only the Discard button does.
- **A scheduled show fires at its new time** after you edit its start time on
  the same day.
- **Undo no longer deletes a preset you just saved.** Saving, updating,
  renaming, reordering and deleting mapping presets are now undo steps of
  their own.
- **Projects take their file's name** instead of staying "Untitled Project"
  after Save As or Open.
- **Warp handles at the canvas edge can be grabbed in full**, and help cards
  no longer pop up over controls right after a click.
- **Screen windows on macOS work again.** A screen window opened on a display
  couldn't be closed (each reopen stacked another one), and it could sit on
  "Waiting for slice" instead of showing its picture. Both are fixed, and Esc
  closes a screen window.
- **Master Warp reaches open screen windows.** Warp edits, undo and redo now
  show on screens that are already open, and the Master Warp handles can be
  grabbed once a screen exists.
- **WLED setups are saved with the project.** Controllers, groups and LED
  effects were lost on reload.
- **Typing in a dropdown no longer fires shortcuts.** Typing B to pick an
  option could black out the output, and T could change the test pattern.
- **One nudge, one move.** The arrow keys now move only the warp you touched
  last, not a screen and Master Warp at once.
- **Bezier layer meshes bulge past their corners.** A curve bent outward past
  the layer's corner pin was cut off at the corners; it now draws in full, with
  the layer's shape and masks following the curve.
- **Overlapping handles can be grabbed.** A mesh point under the move grip, a
  screen corner under a Master Warp corner, and handles at the edge of the
  canvas are all pickable now.
- **Smoother app on macOS when a window is covered.** With the editor window
  hidden behind another window, minimised or on another Space, the app could
  stall for up to a second at a time. The preview now draws off the main thread and pauses while it can't
  be seen.

## Good to know

- **Existing Edge Effects look slightly different.** Colours and motion are
  unchanged, but strokes are drawn at their true width, so they look about
  2 px thinner than before, and an inset stroke sits up to 1.5 px further in.
  Settings that used to be ignored now work: Rotate rotates, and Pulse count,
  Scanner trail, Holographic hue shift, gradient stops and fill opacity take
  effect. Stacked halos are slightly brighter.
- **Edge Effects on empty shapes are brighter.** Shapes with no media used to
  dim their Edge Effects to about a third by mistake; they now draw at full
  strength. Beat-locked dashes, beat wipes and "new colour each beat" follow
  the app's tempo, including a tapped tempo, not only detected audio beats.
- **Heavy stacks cost GPU time.** One simple stroke per layer is cheap; eight
  effects on each of eight layers takes about 35 to 43 ms per frame at 1080p on
  an M1 Max.
- **Deck B in MAP.** Mapping presets on both decks now follow the crossfader,
  so a preset on deck A fades out as you move to B.
- **Art-Net and sACN** have been tested against software senders and
  receivers, not yet on real hardware. Ghost Arcade doesn't answer ArtPoll, so
  desks that discover nodes won't list it; send to its IP address or to
  broadcast instead.
- **The first seconds of a ProRes recording** can be choppier than the rest
  while the encoder starts up, especially at 4K. Start recording a few seconds
  before the moment you need. On Windows, ProRes is encoded in software at up
  to 30 fps.
- **Calibration accuracy depends on the setup.** Spread the points across the
  object and fill as much of the projector's frame as you can. A small object
  far away gives a less exact fit.
- These features were tested on macOS. Windows builds use the same code but
  have not been run on a Windows machine yet.
