# Ghost Arcade v1.9.992

Reliable OSC control, trim-aware video playback, portable VJ media, and textured GLB imports.

## Highlights

- The OSC listener starts with the desktop app, reliably opens the configured UDP port, and reports its active socket status.
- OSC Learn uses an in-app editor instead of Electron's unsupported `prompt()` call.
- OSC parameter paths are validated, normalized, and documented with examples for clips, presets, video transport, layers, and the A/B crossfader.
- MIDI and OSC can control Mapping and VJ video playback, restart, and scrub position.
- Trimmed videos remain inside their selected range and loop back to trim-in rather than source frame zero.
- VJ project saves recover durable local-file references from the media library, reducing unexpected relink prompts after reopening.
- Imported GLB models preserve their embedded materials and textures by default.
- VJ blocks use context-menu removal while clip cells retain their quick remove control.
