# Ghost Arcade v1.9.991

Faster VJ triggering, dependable Stage editing, and more reliable saved Mapping projects.

## Highlights

- VJ videos are decoded and parked at their trim start before activation, preventing the black or shader-only frame previously visible when switching clips.
- Trimmed clips and column launches begin from the selected in point rather than frame zero.
- Unified Stage Designer groups preserve their intended texture order, including layouts containing vertical rows.
- Stage Designer geometry edits support undo, redo, grouped drag history, and canceling an active drag with Escape.
- Multi-selected mapping slices can be stretched from the top, bottom, left, or right while retaining uniform scaling.
- Layer blend modes again use their full-strength compositing behavior, with opacity controlling the layer's final mix.
- Saved Mapping Mode projects recreate video playback after portable media paths are resolved.
