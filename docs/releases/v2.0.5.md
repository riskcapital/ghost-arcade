# Ghost Arcade 2.0.5

Layers keep their own content, and Stage screens land the right way up.

## Layers stopped borrowing each other's content

Changing a layer's content could push that content onto other layers. Update
the shader on a lower mapping layer and the layer above it changed too. In VJ
mode, triggering a column showed one layer's clip on every screen, and in some
columns a layer did not appear at all. The layer thumbnails stayed correct the
whole time, which is what made it so confusing: the app knew what you wanted,
the output did not show it.

Every shader layer draws into one of the core's texture slots. A layer's
rendered frame was never counted as being in use, and the slot it held was
never released when you changed that layer's content. Each clip switch
stranded one. Once they ran out, the next switch took a slot that a live layer
was still drawing from, so two layers shared one frame and whichever rendered
last won.

Slots are now tracked and released as content changes, and the fallback path
can no longer overwrite the shared empty frame that every shader layer reads.

## Stage screens

Applying a stage put its screens in the mirrored half of the canvas and drew
their content upside down. A slice at the top of your stage rendered at the
bottom. It showed in mapping as well as VJ, and the 3D Stage view disagreed
with the flat output because the two used opposite conventions.

Screens now use the same coordinates as every other layer, so the canvas, the
output and the 3D Stage view agree. Stages saved before this release are
converted the first time you open the project, including ones stored inside
stage presets and mapping presets.

The same mismatch was mirroring unified groups: a group with a shader, or one
fed from a VJ layer, drew its content upside down across the whole group. That
is fixed with it.

## Screens tab

A screen's crop took the wrong half of the canvas, so a crop set to the top
showed the bottom. Dragging a screen's top warp handles moved the bottom of the
projected image, and the same applied to the master warp and to warp meshes.
Screen geometry now reaches the compositor in the orientation the Screens tab
shows you.

## three.js and p5.js sources

Sketches now render inside the native engine rather than being blocked by it.
They run in their own offscreen page and upload frames straight to the core, so
they composite, map and record like any other layer, with their parameters and
audio reactivity intact, and with thumbnails in the media tray.

## Fixes

- **Mask layers did nothing.** Clicking on the canvas would not create a mask,
  and once created the mask clipped every layer instead of only the layers
  under it. It now clips the layers below it, the way 1.9 did.
- **A deleted layer flashed back.** Adding a light painting layer briefly
  showed the contents of a lines layer deleted before it, because the new
  layer inherited a texture slot without clearing it.
- **Mesh points ignored the arrow keys.** Nudging a mesh point, with or
  without Shift, moved nothing when focus was still in a panel control.
- **One failing Settings section blacked out the whole panel.**
- **Windows ignored the OS display scale.**
- **Windows said the camera "did not start"** when the OS was blocking
  camera access, instead of saying so.
