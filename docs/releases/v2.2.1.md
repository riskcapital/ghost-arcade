# Ghost Arcade Pro 2.2.1

## Updates without a full download

From this release, Ghost Arcade can update its app code in place. When a release changes only the app and not its engine, the update window offers **Install**: it downloads a small signed package, checks the signature, and asks you to **Restart now** or **Later**. A full installer is only needed when the engine itself changes.

- Every code update is signed, and the app refuses one whose signature or contents do not match.
- If an updated app fails to start twice, Ghost Arcade goes back to the code it shipped with on its own.
- **Settings** shows which code is running and has a button to go back to the built-in code.

## Interactive Studio is back

Interactive Studio is on again by default, with a lot of work behind it.

- **Fluid**, a real liquid simulation for the 3D world, with emitter depth and translucency.
- **Drip Blobs**: paint drawn as one liquid surface, with translucency.
- **Orbit** no longer jumps or flips; Interactive scenes can be VJ clips and have a still preview.
- **3D world:** fires light the real objects in Room and depth modes, embers scatter both ways, flames rise against gravity, paint drips and fire follow the rim of a level top, shadows fit the objects and ball shadows add to the block shadows. Paint gloss is bounded and wet marks look the same at any frame rate.
- **Trigger zones** react per point, re-arm the same way for touch and balls, and fire live bursts.
- **Audio** drives Interactive effects from the real kick and snare, and falls back to 120 BPM when the room is silent.
- The live preview is smoother, the panel's keys stay in the panel, and B (blackout) still reaches the app.

## Auto map

- The phone's solver now runs on the desktop as well, with stripe edges, LiDAR meshes and a diagnostics replay.
- Mapped layers land correctly on turned and warped Screens, paintings warp once, and Verify uses only the current scan.
- Faces are pinned in true perspective; a box's fold stays on its calculated line when the faces snap to the photo; the tops of a cube pyramid are mapped too.
- Each projector's white sets the camera exposure that is held for its scan, and a run can be stopped.
- The room scan places the projector more robustly and says why when it cannot use a scan.
- A phone that drops off ends its scan on the desktop straight away, and large calibrations travel in pieces.

## Audio Lab

- **Undo and redo across Audio Lab.** Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z (Ctrl+Y on Windows) undo effect changes (add, delete, reorder, bypass, settings), drum machine edits, clip notes and Looper record, overdub, draw, erase, quantise and Clear. A knob or fader drag is one step.
- **One beat in every set.** Each set's Beat module shows and edits the drum machine's beat, with one Play and Stop. The drums sound the same on every tab, through the drum machine's effects.
- **Recorded drum hits get their rows.** Tap pads while recording and each new sound gets a row on the drum machine that scrolls into view, so its hits can be edited or deleted.
- **A Looper that starts when you play.** Press Record and take your time: the take begins on your first note, lined up with the bar nearest it, so there is no gap at the start of the loop. Choose **Start: On first note** or **On next bar**, with an optional count-in.
- **Clear Looper buttons.** They fire on the press. Armed pulses red, recording is solid red with the bar count, overdub is amber and playing is green. Every column has an Undo, and Shift+S saves.
- New loops sit on the playing loops' grid from their first pass, keep the sound you played them with, and show their notes in the lane while you record.
- Deleting a note in the loop editor deletes just that note.
- Each Looper cell has its play/record button on top with the controls lined up underneath, and the Looper scrolls instead of clipping in the lower panel.
- The macro editor's effect picker opens in front, and a sound menu no longer swallows the note keys.

## Timeline and show control

- Ripple delete, moving a block to another row and splitting keep the block's lines; dragging several blocks moves each block's lines once.
- Cue markers fire on every vamp, loop and jump pass.
- Live Key's Discard puts back what it can, content before its settings, and Update keys everything before letting go.
- Takeover, Perform standbys and Live sections hand pieces back cleanly when the other document opens or the show resets.
- Timecode rolls on through midnight, and LTC stays steady over a long show.
- In VJ mode and Audio Lab, a pill names the Timeline playing underneath and can pause it.
- On a 720p laptop the stage keeps its strip above the tray, and Perform takes at most 40% of the window.
- Where motions step on the song's kicks, and LED speed, colour count and pattern lines move through their real values.

## Mapping

- **Group effects work in Mapping mode.** Effects on a group now render on each of its layers, after the layer's own effects. Override still replaces the layer's effects.

## Faster

- Effects use a pipeline tuned to each effect, intermediate effect steps skip work they never needed, and the render core reuses its GPU objects from frame to frame.
- Mesh-warped layers and Screens search less of the mesh per pixel, a still mesh costs nothing to resend, and a deleted Screen frees its mesh.
- Editing a value on a playing VJ clip no longer resends the whole grid.
