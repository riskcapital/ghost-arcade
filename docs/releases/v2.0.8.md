# Ghost Arcade 2.0.8

Video now decodes on your graphics card, loops without a hitch, and scratches
under a mouse or a MIDI knob.

## Video plays on the GPU

Clips are decoded by the graphics hardware built into your computer and handed
straight to the renderer, instead of being decoded on the CPU and copied through
memory frame by frame. On macOS that is VideoToolbox with Metal. On Windows it is
Media Foundation with Direct3D 12. It leaves far more room for layers, effects
and outputs, and it is what makes the rest of this release possible.

Clips the hardware path can't handle, such as video with transparency, switch
to the compatibility decoder automatically. You don't need to change anything.

## Loops are clean, and triggers are ready

- **Seamless loops.** A looping clip goes from its last frame straight back to
  its first. Before, each pass could freeze on the last frame and then jump
  ahead, and it got worse the longer a clip ran.
- **No more green first pass.** A triggered clip shows its real picture from
  the first frame, with no green, stale or blank frame before it.
- **Prepared retriggers.** Clips on the VJ grid keep their opening frames ready
  on the GPU, so refiring a clip or a whole column starts right away. A clip
  that hasn't played for a while may still take a moment to prepare.
- **Clips start at their in point.** A video dropped onto a mapping layer starts
  at the beginning of its range, not partway through.
- **Smoother under load.** Diagnostic snapshots that could stall the interface
  for a moment are now off by default, and the app keeps macOS from putting
  playback to sleep in the background.

## Scrub, step and scratch

- Drag a clip's timeline to scrub through it.
- Step one frame at a time with the new previous and next frame buttons, or the
  Left and Right arrow keys on the timeline.
- Scratch with a MIDI knob or fader: MIDI-learn the video timeline, and moving
  the control scrubs the clip and holds the frame where you stop.

## Longer effect chains

Effect chains now take up to 16 effects, up from 4: a clip and its layer
share one chain of 16, and the composition has its own 16. Before, adding a
fifth effect silently switched off the whole chain. Now any effect past the
limit is skipped with a warning in the effects panel, and the first 16 keep
working.

## Fixes

- **Toolbar on smaller screens.** On a 1280 px laptop screen the right end of
  the top toolbar, including Connect Mobile, ran off the edge of the window. The
  toolbar now tightens its spacing and shortens labels as needed so everything
  stays on screen.

## Good to know

Windows hardware playback has been tested on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070. AMD and
Intel graphics use the same path but have not been through the same testing
yet. If a clip ever plays incorrectly, it can be forced onto the compatibility
decoder by starting Ghost Arcade with the environment variable
`GA_NATIVE_VIDEO_BACKEND=software`. Please tell us if you need it.

HAP playback, reverse playback and HDR video are not part of this release.
