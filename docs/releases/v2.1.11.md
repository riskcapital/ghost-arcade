# Ghost Arcade Pro 2.1.11

## Edit p5.js sketches in the app

- **Import p5.js sketch** in the Media tray's JS tab takes a p5.js Web Editor username (to browse that account's public sketches), a link to one sketch, or a dropped zip, folder or set of sketch files. Each sketch is previewed before it is added, and is kept as it was written.
- **Edit code** (in the Layer panel, on a p5 tile with `</>`, or in the tray's sketch controls) opens the sketch's own files as tabs, with the layer's live picture beside them. Edits reach the layer a moment after you stop typing, and errors are shown in plain words at the file and line you wrote. Learning mode explains the p5 ideas a sketch uses, with links to the p5.js reference and tutorials, and tips offer one-click fixes that keep the code plain p5.js.
- **Save** checks the code first. Code that does not compile, or that throws when it runs, is not saved, and the editor says where and what is wrong. Typed code is kept as a draft, so it comes back if the app closes before you save.
- Saving reaches every copy of the sketch: duplicated layers, saved compositions, VJ clips and stage presets.
- Sketches play on layers and thumbnails with their images, sounds and fonts and with p5.sound. A loop that never ends is stopped with an error on its line instead of freezing the page (`// noprotect` turns this off).
- Three.js and p5.js pages hear the show through `window.ghostAudio`: level, bass, mid, treble, beat, kick, snare, BPM and more, about 30 times a second.
- Autosave and stage presets keep sketches whole, however large their files.

## Build a show around a song

- **File → New Show from Audio…**, the welcome screen's **Make a show to a song**, or dropping a song on the workspace opens one dialog. Keep the current look for the whole song, spread chosen presets across it, or let the song's tempo, beats and section changes bring in a new preset at each change, on the beat.
- Show audio plays any song: formats Chromium cannot play (AIFF, WMA, ALAC and others) are converted once and play from the copy.
- **Render to Video** includes the show's soundtrack.
- The Show timeline follows the song's own clock, so long shows no longer drift from the music. A show that does not loop can hold its last look, go back to the start, or black out at the end.
- The Show tray picks which sound card the show plays on.
- VJ mode shows a compact soundtrack bar while a show with audio plays.

## See how each video plays

- Videos in the Media tray, on layers and in VJ clip cells carry a badge when they decode in software (**SW**, with the reason) or cannot play (**!**). **Convert for smooth playback** makes a copy that every Mac and PC decodes in hardware and points the show at it.
- **File → Video Converter** has a **Playback** preset for the same.
- **Settings → Video Decoding** lists what plays in hardware on this computer.
- Videos are no longer decoded twice in the desktop app, videos on small slices really decode at the slice size, and the software fallback uses the hardware where it can.
- On macOS, HEVC and H.264 files tagged `hev1` or `avc3` now play on the hardware decoder.
- Mapping layers accept every video the renderer plays, including ProRes, HAP, DNx and 10-bit files, and say so when a file cannot load.

## A Preview monitor for the VJ

The **Preview** button in the VJ toolbar opens a fullscreen monitor on a display of your own, beside the Live Output. It shows the program large with Deck A and Deck B beside it (or the program alone), with a status line for tempo, block, mode, crossfader and recording. Unplug its display and it closes; plug it back in and it returns.

## Saving and undo you can rely on

- Quitting asks to save when there are unsaved changes, and every edit now counts: effects, shader settings, blend modes, masks, Screens and VJ clips.
- Opening a project asks about unsaved work first. **File → New** starts from nothing the last project owned.
- The whole output stage (dome, output rotation and crop, edge blend and output grade) and every setting on a layer's media source are saved with the project. A Milkdrop layer reopens on its saved preset.
- One undo takes back one thing. Ctrl/Cmd+Z in a text field undoes the typing.
- Controllers, automation and modulation never make undo steps; a recorded take is one step. Undoing an edit no longer takes back a controller, show or preset change made before it.
- Playing a show no longer counts as editing it, so a played show closes without a save prompt.
- On Windows, saves succeed while OneDrive, Defender or the preview pane holds the file.

## Mapping points

- Snapping works in screen pixels and only to what is drawn: other layers' outlines, the canvas edges and nearby grid lines. One **Snap** switch sits in the status bar.
- The arrow keys move any selected point (Shift ten pixels, Alt a tenth), on every kind of layer, Screen, Master Warp and the Stage Designer.
- Shift drags points finely everywhere, and points no longer jump when grabbed.
- Mesh warp moves the content inside a Custom Shape and leaves the drawn outline where it is.
- Auto map turns curved surfaces measured by the phone into mesh layers that follow the surface.

## Outputs and Screens

- Screens find their projector again after Windows renumbers displays, and a Screen whose projector is unplugged no longer opens on another display.
- The Live Output opens on the display chosen for it, and leaves fullscreen when closed.
- On Windows, Screens fill their display at any scale, and each Screen presents at its own display's refresh.
- Frames are paced to the output display, and minimising the Live Output no longer stops the picture.

## Timing

- Quantised clip launches land on the beat, not after it.
- The mapping sequencer stays on the beat, and the VJ sequencer keeps its step when the tempo changes.
- A new visual never stalls the output, and playing clips keep moving while the editor is busy.

## Look and layout

- A new welcome screen.
- One **Mapping | VJ** switch, the same size and in the same place in both modes, with the current mode in silver.
- The mapping top bar keeps the waveform and its audio settings in the bar, with Tap and the BPM behind one BPM button and a **Mobile** button with a phone icon.
- The top bar keeps its height in a narrow window, and the VJ header stays on one row when audio starts.
- Sentence-case labels throughout, and every language pack now covers the whole interface.
- The app icon carries a small PRO label.

## Reliability

- If the renderer cannot start, the workspace says why, with **Retry** and **Open log**; a renderer that stops is restarted.
- The renderer starts on Windows PCs without the Visual C++ runtime and on Windows N editions.
- **Settings → Diagnostics → Open log folder**, and the last three logs are kept.
- A recording the renderer refuses puts the REC button back.

## Fixes

- Edits to a VJ clip still playing from another block are saved in that block, and deleting a block no longer disturbs deck B.
- Performer no longer renders in the background, and its effects stay on its own clips.
- On macOS, menus under the title strip take clicks, and the editor keeps the menu bar when an output opens.
- On Windows, only the top bar's empty space moves the window, and a second launch hands over to the running app.
- Deck monitors keep a portrait project's shape.
- Ableton setup reads the installed Live version correctly.
