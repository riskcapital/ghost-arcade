# Ghost Arcade 2.0.7

Screen FX work in VJ STAGE mode, mesh and corner warp work together, every
MIDI mapping in one place, and particles that light, focus and film
themselves.

## VJ STAGE mode

**Screen FX did nothing in VJ mode.** Strobes, chases and pulses on your
Screens barely reached the output, if at all. The effect was being applied to
one update per frame while the decks, keyframes and sequencer sent their own
undimmed updates many times a second, so the dimmed one almost never got drawn.
Screen FX are now part of every update and run at the rate you set, whether
your Screens follow a deck row or the VJ Mix. Turning an effect off puts the
Screens back at full brightness.

**Triggering deck B flashed the row underneath.** With a clip playing on a row
of deck A, triggering deck B on the same row could show the row below it for a
moment. On Screens that follow the VJ Mix it was the lower row for a frame; on
Screens that follow the row directly it could be a black flash, longer the
first time in a session. The renderer now draws each row's crossfade before
the mix that reads it, and the crossfade is prepared when the app starts
rather than at the moment you trigger. Both are covered by tests that fail on
2.0.6.

## Warping

**Mesh warp stays on when you switch back to Corner warp.** It used to be one
or the other: warp a mesh, switch to Corner to pin the corners, and the mesh
disappeared from the output. Now the two work together. Corner and Mesh only
choose which handles you are editing, so you can shape a mesh and then corner
pin the whole warped picture, and it follows. Corner mode has a Reset Mesh
button while a warped mesh is still applied.

**Windows: the picture sat off its warp box.** On a Windows display set above
100% scaling, the image in the editor drew smaller than and up and to the left
of its warp handles, which made it hard to see where each corner was. The
preview now follows your display scale and sits exactly under the handles,
including after moving the window to a monitor with a different scale.

## MIDI mappings in Settings

Settings, MIDI now lists every mapping you have learned, grouped by control and
named by layer and effect, so four mapped effects no longer read as four rows
called "Intensity". Remove a single mapping, clear everything a pad is bound
to, or clear them all. Find a control highlights the pad you press without
firing what it drives, which is the quick way to find and unlearn a pad you
mapped deep inside an effect. Mappings whose effect is not in the open project
are marked instead of hidden.

## Flythrough and Pixel Particles

- **Lit grains.** Particles can render as small lit spheres with a light
  direction, material and soft shadows.
- **Focus.** A focus distance and aperture blur particles in front of and
  behind the focal plane.
- **Motion.** Limit Wander keeps particles near home, and movement in your
  source can push particles harder. Flythrough's flow is smoother and cheaper.
- **Auto Camera.** A camera that plans its own shots: wide, push in, detail,
  rack focus, pull back. It picks subjects from where your picture has the
  most going on, with a pace, closeness and variation, and it can move on the
  beat.
