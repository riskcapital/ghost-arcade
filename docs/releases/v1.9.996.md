# Ghost Arcade v1.9.996

The 1.9 line works with the Ghost Arcade phone app, and picks up a large part
of the 2.0 interface.

## Pair your phone

Connect Mobile now shows a pairing code beside the QR code. Scan the QR code,
or type the code into the phone app, and the phone stays paired after a
restart. Only paired devices can reach the remote, which matters on a shared
venue network. Reset pairing disconnects every phone at once.

Phones that connected to 1.9.995 without a code need to scan the new QR code
once.

## From the phone app

- **Visuals from the phone.** The phone app's Studio can send its camera,
  depth and Interactive visuals to the desktop, where they arrive as a source
  you can put on any layer.
- **Auto-map.** With a Screen open on a projector, the phone photographs
  stripe patterns on it and finds the flat surfaces in view. Each one arrives
  as a layer already pinned in place.
- **Paintings.** A painting on the wall arrives with its own photo on its
  layer, lined up and cropped. Cut it into shapes and each patch of colour
  becomes its own mapping layer, with a slider for how much detail to keep.
- **Calibration files.** A calibration exported from the phone opens under
  Connect Mobile, Phone mapping and calibration, and creates a layer on the
  surface you traced.
- **Scans.** A scan sent from the phone is saved on the desktop after you
  accept it and can be added as a point cloud layer.

## Clips play their sound

Video clips on the VJ deck now play their audio. Trigger a clip and you hear
it; stop it and it goes quiet. Each clip has its own Volume, Pan and Mute in
the clip panel, a row's Mute and Solo apply to its sound, the crossfader
fades sound along with the picture, and a master level appears beside the
audio input picker while a clip is playing. To keep a clip silent, mute it.

## Performer is a plugin

Performer has moved from a button in the VJ top bar to the plugin list. Drag
it onto any cell and fire that cell to open it on that row. It no longer
writes into the first column of the row, and its effects stay with Performer
instead of being added to the row, so an ordinary clip on the same row plays
clean. The VJ top bar now stays on one line at any window width.

## VJ deck

- **Missing files.** A clip whose file has moved shows a **?**. Search a folder
  to relink every moved clip at once, or choose a file for one clip.
- **Fill a row.** Drag a clip's bottom corner sideways to put it in every
  column you pass. It keeps playing when you fire another column.
- **Reorder rows.** Drag a row by the handle on its LIVE thumbnail.
- **Stop all** lights up and pulses while everything is stopped.
- **Autopilot.** The **A** button on a row makes it move on by itself after a
  number of beats or video loops: next, previous, random, first, last or a set
  column. Empty cells are skipped.
- **Cue points.** Eight pads per video clip. An empty pad saves the playhead,
  a saved pad jumps to it, shift-click replaces it.
- **Layer groups.** GROUPS in the top bar gives a range of adjacent rows one
  level fader and its own column launches; a group launch leaves the other
  rows playing.

## Run a show

A new **Show** button beside Keyframes opens the show timeline.

- **Show timeline.** Arrange presets and audio tracks on a timeline. Where two
  presets touch you can add a Dissolve, Dip to Black or Additive transition.
  Dip to Black runs across the whole transition; Dissolve and Additive blend
  from the frozen outgoing picture into the new preset once it takes over.
- **Cue list.** A cue can recall a preset, fire a deck clip or column, stop
  the deck, black out, move a macro, a layer's opacity or any control with a
  fade time, drive the timeline, or command a projector. GO, Back, follow
  times and markers on the timeline.
- **Schedule.** Daily or weekly start and stop times with date exceptions,
  under Settings, Show Control.
- **Timecode.** Chase LTC from an audio input or MTC over MIDI.
- **Start at boot.** Start with the computer, open a show and run it with no
  one at the machine.
- **Projectors.** PJLink power, shutter and input control with status, from
  cues and the schedule.

## Lighting

- **DMX input.** A lighting desk on Art-Net or sACN can drive layer opacity,
  effect parameters, macros, the crossfader and clip or column triggers.
  Settings, Integrations, DMX Input. Off until you switch it on.
- **Pixel mapping.** Send regions of the picture to LED fixtures on Art-Net or
  sACN nodes: strips, matrices and custom layouts, RGB and RGBW, test
  patterns, and the same LED FX and LED groups as WLED. Settings,
  Integrations, Pixel Mapping.

## Project Media

File, Project Media lists every local file the project uses, shows which are
missing, relinks them, and collects the project and its media into one
portable folder.

## Interface

- **Interface size** in Settings, Appearance: 75% to 200%.
- The 2.0 look: its colours, sliders and controls, typefaces, logo, app icon and
  start-up screen, and a third theme, Neo Industrial.
- Help that opens on the panel you are looking at.
- Type sums into number fields: `1920/2`, `45+90`.
- AUTO tempo.
- Save and reuse effect chains.
- A MIDI mapping inspector, controller lights, and OSC output and feedback.
- Assign macros from the control you want to drive.
- Save and recall Screen setups.

## AI control

Settings, OSC has an **AI control (MCP)** switch. When on, an AI client on
this computer can read the app's state, set any control and look at the
output. It is off by default and every request needs the token shown there.

## Safer file access

The app now only writes where you chose in a save dialog, and only reads
media you picked or dropped, media a project you opened refers to, and its
own folders. Media from your last session is carried over the first time
1.9.996 starts. If a clip shows a **?** after updating, relink it from the
deck.

## Welcome screen, tour and templates

Every launch opens on a welcome screen: take a guided tour, start from a
template, open a project, or start blank. Press 1 to 4 to choose, or Esc for a
blank project. The tour has paths for VJ, projection mapping and musicians, and
each step opens the panel it talks about. The templates (Band Stage, Club VJ,
Psychedelic, Mapping Starter) build a working deck in one click.

## VJ mode, laid out as in 2.0

- The deck controls sit above the deck, and the toolbars and layer controls
  stay pinned while the clip grid scrolls.
- The deck bar has tempo detection, nudge, resync and Audio Out, and the VJ
  header follows the 2.0 layout.
- **Live Stage Edit**: edit the stage's Screens, warps and VJ routing while
  the show plays.
- Riding a fader or the crossfader redraws only what it moves, so the
  interface stays smooth during a fade.

## Mapping and Screens

- **Projector calibration**, **angled overlap blending** and **Screen masks**.
- **Bezier meshes** on layers, Screens and the Master Warp.
- A layer shape's edge outline traces the same shape its mask draws, and
  star layers draw again.
- Screen outputs show the right colours; they were converted twice before.

## Shaders and effects

- Thirty library shaders that played black now compile and run.
- **Defocus Bokeh** can keep a round area or a band sharp and soften the
  picture toward the edges.

## Runs on more computers

- Where a computer has no WebGPU, the editor falls back to WebGL instead of
  failing.
- On Linux, Ghost Arcade renders on graphics drivers Chromium has blocklisted,
  and Launch at login works.
- The phone link sends nothing while no phone is connected.

## Updates

1.9.996 checks for newer releases of this line, including **Ghost Arcade
Studio**, its next version, and tells you when one is out: a window opens once
for each new version, and the version in the status bar offers it after that.
It never offers the 2.x line, which needs a newer kind of graphics card.
