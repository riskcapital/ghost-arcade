# Ghost Arcade v1.9.995

The whole top toolbar fits on smaller screens.

## Toolbar buttons were cut off

On a screen narrower than about 1500 pixels, such as a 13" laptop, the right
end of the top toolbar ran off the edge of the window. Stage, Settings and
Connect Mobile were out of reach, and there was no way to open Settings at
all. Turning audio on made it worse, because the tempo widget takes more room.

The toolbar now fits itself to the window. It tightens its spacing first, then
shortens a few labels and shows Stage Sim, Map Sim and Stage as icons, and
only if that is still not enough lets the middle buttons scroll sideways.
Every button keeps its tooltip, and Settings and the right-hand controls always
stay on screen. On a wide screen nothing changes.

1.9.995 is 1.9.994 with this fix and nothing else.
