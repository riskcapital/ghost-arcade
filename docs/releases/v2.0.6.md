# Ghost Arcade 2.0.6

Settings opens on OSC without blacking out the app.

## The OSC tab blacked out the app

Opening Settings and clicking OSC turned the window black, with nothing
clickable and no way out other than force quitting. Some people saw it as a
blank tab, others as the whole app going dark. Reopening Settings landed back
on OSC and went black again, because the panel remembers the section you were
last in.

Nothing had crashed. The OSC settings were drawn and in place the entire time,
underneath a black rectangle. Two of the switches in that section were built
with a style name the app does not define, and each one stretched to cover the
window instead of sitting in its row. The rectangle took every click too,
which is what made the app feel frozen rather than merely blank.

The switches sit in their rows again, and a switch that loses its styling now
draws nothing at all rather than covering the screen. AI control and Send
feedback also show whether they are on, which they never did.

This was in every 2.0.3, 2.0.4 and 2.0.5 build. If you use OSC or MCP, or you
simply clicked through the Settings tabs, this is the one that caught you.
