# Ghost Arcade 2.0.9

HAP playback, clip audio, VJ groups, color LUTs and multi-parameter macros,
plus faster, steadier clip launches on Windows.

## HAP and more video formats

- **HAP on the GPU.** HAP, HAP Alpha and HAP Q clips play as compressed
  textures straight on the graphics card, on macOS and Windows. A graphics card
  that can't use those textures falls back to the compatibility decoder.
- **More converter formats.** The Video Converter now writes H.264, HAP, HAP
  Alpha, HAP Q, ProRes 422 HQ and ProRes 4444 Alpha. HAP Alpha and ProRes 4444
  Alpha keep transparency. The Windows installer includes the HAP encoder, so
  there is nothing extra to install.
- **Better imports.** Videos the preview can't decode itself, such as HAP, now
  import with a thumbnail and duration.
- **Project Media.** File > Project Media finds missing files, relinks them, or
  collects a show and all of its media into one folder.

## Clip audio

Clip audio now plays through the native renderer in the VJ workspace, and it is
on by default. Each clip has volume, pan and mute, each layer has volume and
pan, and the A/B crossfader mixes audio at a constant loudness. Audio Out
chooses the output device and sets the master volume and mute.

Recordings don't include clip audio yet.

## Playback and launching

- **Reverse and Bounce** playback in VJ and Mapping. Reverse works on clips
  played by the hardware or HAP decoder.
- **Cue pads.** Each VJ video clip has eight. An empty pad stores the current
  position, a stored pad jumps back to it, and Shift-click replaces it. Pads can
  be learned to MIDI and the keyboard.
- **Beat-synced loops** on hardware and HAP clips stay in phase with the tempo,
  adjusting speed by no more than 4%.
- **Transitions** from 0 to 10 seconds in 10 styles, set per layer. Each clip
  can use the layer's transition or its own.
- **Quantized columns.** Column launches follow QUANT: they wait for the beat,
  and pressing again cancels.
- **Trigger modes:** Normal, Toggle and Piano.
- **Layer controls on the row.** Lock; Autopilot, which moves to the next,
  previous, first, last, a random or a specific clip after a number of beats or
  loops; Ignore Column Trigger; and Fader Start, which restarts the video when
  the layer fades up from zero. Clips can override the last two.
- **Tempo nudge and RESYNC** buttons.

## Effects and macros

- **Effect-chain presets.** Save and load a chain, and import or export preset
  libraries, in VJ and Mapping.
- **Arithmetic in number fields.** Type something like 120/2 into effect
  values, the BPM field or a transition duration.
- **More modulation.** Auto modulation gets a Driver (Free, Beat sync,
  Crossfader A/B or Clip position) and a Curve. Clip and composition effects
  can now use Audio, LFO, Beat and Auto. New keyframe curves: Sine, Exponential
  Out, Bounce Out and Elastic Out.
- **Color LUTs.** Load a 3D .cube file (2 to 65 points per axis) and set its
  strength. The LUT is saved inside the project and in presets.
- **Multi-parameter macros.** Each of the eight knobs across the top can drive
  several effect parameters, each with its own start and end values and an
  invert option. A macro's effect bundle can now use any effect, including
  blur.

## VJ groups and screens

- **VJ Groups** combine adjacent rows with a shared level, blend mode and effect
  chain, and launch columns inside the group only. Group controls can be learned
  to MIDI. Groups work in Mix and Stage.
- **Shared group feeds.** A Screen's slice source can be a group. The group is
  rendered once, and every slice using it shares that feed.
- **Up to 32 screens**, up from 8. A layout that doesn't fit is flagged in the
  Screens panel instead of being dropped.

## Control and help

- **Help beside the controls.** Hover over or tab to a control to see what it
  does. F1 moves to its documentation link, Esc closes the card, and Settings >
  Tooltips turns the cards off.
- **Controller lights** for the APC40 mkII, APC mini mk2, Launchpad X,
  Launchpad Mini mk3 and learned pads.
- **New MIDI and OSC targets:** cue pads, tempo nudge and resync, autopilot,
  clip, layer and master audio, and group level, columns and effects.
- **Ableton Link.** Playback now follows the Link clock when Link is on.
- **Phone remote pairing.** The remote now needs a pairing code, so other
  people on the venue Wi-Fi can't take control. The code is built into the QR
  code, and Reset pairing disconnects every paired phone at once.
- **Performer mode** worlds have a Color scheme choice with eight palettes.

## Faster launches on Windows

Clips on Windows launch faster and more consistently. Ghost Arcade keeps up to
16 clips ready, up from 6, and reuses them when you retrigger instead of
reopening them. All rows of a column appear together, and hovering over a
column gets it ready to launch.

## Fixes

- Stage Screens showing a VJ row or the VJ Mix now include that row's effects.
- Clip and layer effect opacity works. Effects were always applied at 100%.
- Mapping composition effects no longer appear over VJ output, and VJ
  composition effects now show in Maps.
- Macro effect bundles keep all of their effects, not only color adjustments.
- WLED works with the native renderer. LEDs leave realtime mode two seconds
  after output stops, and LED effects follow a manually entered BPM.
- Strong or folded mesh and corner warps no longer show holes or a duplicated
  image.
- Screens past the eighth are no longer silently dropped.
- The render core no longer keeps running after the app is force quit.
- Starting Ghost Arcade no longer closes other software using port 9001.
- Turning on Ableton Link no longer pushes Ghost Arcade's manual BPM to the
  other Link peers.
- The Mapping composition panel scrolls in short windows.

## Good to know

- **Clip audio is on by default.** Projects with audio in their clips will now
  play it. Mute a clip, or use Audio Out, to silence it.
- **Pair phones again.** Phones paired with an earlier version need to scan the
  new QR code once.
- **Not in this release:** recording clip audio, recording single layers or
  Screens, recording with transparency, Bezier warp, per-Screen polygon masks,
  mask painting, per-effect blend modes (the two Mapping selectors are disabled
  for now), image-sequence clips and HDR.
- Windows hardware playback has been tested most on NVIDIA graphics; AMD and
  Intel have had less testing. If a clip plays incorrectly, start Ghost Arcade
  with the environment variable `GA_NATIVE_VIDEO_BACKEND=software` and tell us
  about it.
- HAP conversion has been checked on Apple Silicon Macs and Windows. Intel Macs
  have not been checked yet.
