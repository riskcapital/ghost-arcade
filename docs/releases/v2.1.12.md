# Ghost Arcade Pro 2.1.12

## The Timeline

A new **Timeline** tab sits beside the Show tab. The Show tab stays simple: presets and music. The Timeline is the score for the whole stage.

- **One row per mapping layer.** Drop a video, image, shader or sketch on a row and it plays on that piece for the block's span. Click a block to edit it in the normal Properties and Shader panels. A row with no blocks shows the piece's own look; a row with blocks hides its piece between them. Add layers to a piece as sub-rows that draw over it, with their own blend.
- **Edit blocks the way a video editor does.** Move, trim, split, crossfade, copy-drag (Alt), ripple delete (Shift+Delete), undo and redo, snapping to the beat grid, block edges, the playhead and markers.
- **Looks row.** Drag a Look from the Looks gallery onto the Timeline and the shapes wear it for the block's span.
- **Control lines.** Any setting of any layer can have a line: opacity, blend, visibility, corner pins, mesh points, move, scale and rotate, shader and effect settings, sketch controls, video speed. Draw a line with the pen or with points and curves, let it follow the music (level, bass, kick and more), an LFO, a MIDI knob, an OSC address or an Ableton knob, or record it.
- **Where lines** move a look or a block's content across the pieces in an order you choose.
- **LED rows** drive WLED controllers: colours, gradients, chases, effects, or following the projected picture.
- **Screen FX and Effects rows** run stage effects and composition effects for a span.
- **Sound rows**: songs with level, fades, ducking and effects, included in Render to Video, and blocks that fire Ableton Live clips.
- **Sections and GO.** Name the parts of a song. A section can play on, hold for GO (Enter), loop until GO, jump back after a number of passes, or hand the stage to the VJ decks until GO. Sections can come from Live's locators.
- **Phrases** launch a section as an audio and visual phrase on the next bar, from pads in VJ mode or on the Timeline, firing Live clips and scenes ahead of time.
- **Auto show.** Drop a song, press ✦ Auto show, and the Timeline builds a whole show on your existing mapping: piece groups, content from the built-in catalog or your library, looks, Screen FX and builds that follow the song's sections and style. Shuffle until you like it; one undo takes it back.
- **Follow the clock.** The Timeline follows MIDI clock, Ableton Link, Live's song position and timecode, with a tempo map and beat grid of its own.
- **Live takeover.** Shift+L (or LIVE on a row) hands a row to a VJ deck row, the mix, a group, dropped media or black; R hands everything back, now or on the next beat, bar, phrase or marker.

## Record what you do, live

- **Record armed, Follow edits on, play**: everything you change in Mapping, the panels or with a controller is written onto the Timeline as you do it. Settings become lines, one undo step per gesture; putting other content on a piece becomes a block on its row, written over what was there.
- **Record on an empty Timeline.** Armed, Play starts on an empty Timeline and the playhead rolls on past the end, so there is always time to record into.
- **Live edit with the playhead parked.** Armed with Follow edits on, an edit lands at the playhead: a setting is keyed there, and new content on a piece cuts its block at the playhead.
- Engines that move values on their own (audio and LFO modulation, Auto, Music motion, sections, cues and takeovers) are never recorded as your edits. Touch and Latch decide what a recorded setting does when you let go.

## Keys a video editor already knows

- **J K L** play backwards (press J again for 2×, 4×, 8×), stop and play.
- **I** and **O** set in and out points, drawn on the ruler and saved with the Timeline; with Loop on, play loops between them. Shift+I / Shift+O go to them, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+X clears them.
- **↑ ↓** jump to the previous or next edit point; **← →** step a frame when nothing is selected.
- A **Tool** menu beside Snap picks the pointer's tool on blocks: **Select** (V), **Razor** (C: click a block to cut it there), **Ripple** (Shift+B: drag an edge and every later block on the row moves with it; B stays Blackout), **Roll** (N: drag the cut between two touching blocks) and **Slip** (Y: drag a block to play a different part of its media in place). Ctrl/Cmd+K splits at the playhead.
- **Ctrl/Cmd+G** groups blocks across rows so they select, move and copy as one; **Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+G** ungroups.
- Control lines take **Bezier** curves: right-click a point, choose Bezier, and drag its handles to shape the curve, past the change to overshoot.
- **Q** and **W** trim the block under the playhead to it. **= - \\** zoom in, out and to fit. **U** shows or hides a row's lines. **S** toggles snapping.
- The playhead line runs down every row, however far the rows are scrolled.

## Rehearse and record the stage

- Stage Sim plays the Timeline as a rehearsal, records it with sound and an orbiting or shot-based camera, and renders a rehearsal video. Recordings capture a new picture every frame, so short flashes are kept.
- A stage-camera lane on the Timeline cuts between Demo Reel shots.

## Running unattended

- **Install mode** runs a show unattended: a schedule, a watchdog that restarts what stops, resume after a restart, and a health log.

## Music motion

- **Music motion** on a shader layer or clip moves the shader's own time with the music: slow in quiet parts, quicker as it builds, a push on each hit. Shaders made for it carry a small waveform mark.

## Find latest

- **Find latest** brings in community shaders, p5.js sketches and three.js pages, signed so they cannot be changed on the way.
