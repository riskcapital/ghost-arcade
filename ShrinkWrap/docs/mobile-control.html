Mobile Control

Control your entire show from any phone or tablet. Walk around the venue, adjust mappings, trigger clips, and fine-tune parameters from anywhere on site.

Overview

Shrink Wrap uses a distributed architecture where your mobile device handles the control interface while your desktop manages all rendering and projector output. Communication happens over a low-latency WebSocket connection via WiFi.

Any device with a web browser can serve as a controller — phones, tablets, laptops, or other computers. No app installation required.

Connecting

  1. Make sure your desktop and mobile device are on the same WiFi network
  2. In Shrink Wrap, click the Mobile button in the toolbar
  3. A QR code and URL appear on screen
  4. Scan the QR code with your phone or enter the URL in a browser
  5. The mobile control interface loads automatically
Tip

Bookmark the URL on your phone for quick access at future sessions. The IP address may change between networks, but it stays consistent within a session.

Hotspot Mode

For field installations where WiFi isn't available, use your phone's mobile hotspot:

  1. Enable Personal Hotspot on your phone
  2. Connect your desktop/laptop to the hotspot network
  3. Launch the mobile control connection in Shrink Wrap
  4. Open the control URL on the same phone (or another device connected to the hotspot)
Note

No internet connection is required. The WebSocket connection is purely local between your devices. Hotspot mode works in completely offline environments.

Touch Interface

The mobile control interface is optimized for touch interaction with large, finger-friendly controls:

  • Layer selector — Switch between layers with large tap targets
  • Parameter sliders — Touch and drag to adjust values
  • Toggle buttons — Tap to enable/disable features
  • Live preview — Real-time thumbnail of the current output

Warp Control

One of the most powerful mobile features — adjust projection warp points while standing at the projection surface:

  1. Select the layer to adjust on your mobile
  2. Switch to Warp view
  3. Touch and drag warp corner or mesh points
  4. The projection adjusts in real-time as you drag

This workflow is essential for live installations. Instead of running back and forth between your computer and the projection surface, you can make adjustments while looking directly at the result.

VJ Clip Triggering

In VJ mode, the mobile interface shows the clip launcher grid. Tap clips to trigger them, just like on the desktop interface. This lets you perform from anywhere in the venue.

  • See all loaded clips with thumbnail previews
  • Tap to trigger clips on any layer
  • Active clips are highlighted
  • Switch compositions from mobile

Parameter Control

Most layer parameters are accessible from the mobile interface. Adjust shader values, media playback, effects intensity, opacity, and blend modes without touching the desktop.

Tip

During a performance, keep your laptop at the tech desk running the renders while you roam the venue with your phone. Trigger clips, adjust effects, and fine-tune the projection from anywhere on site.
